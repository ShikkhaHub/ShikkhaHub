# Content Strategy & Production System

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: Institution Guides (40% of content)

Focus on specific institutions with deep, valuable content.

**Format Options**:
- YouTube: "Complete Guide to [University]" (7-10 min)
- Blog: 1,500-2,000 word guide
- Instagram Series: 10-15 carousel posts
- TikTok: 5-7 quick clips from the content

**Content Structure**:
1. Overview: Location, type, founded, notable alumni
2. Academics: Programs, admission requirements, curriculum
3. Campus Life: Facilities, clubs, sports, social life
4. Placement: Companies, salary range, placement rate
5. Fees & Scholarships: Costs, financial aid, scholarships
6. Reviews: Student testimonials, ratings
7. Application: Step-by-step how to apply
8. Q&A: Common questions answered

**Production Pipeline**:
- Research: 1 hour (institution website, Glassdoor, alumni networks)
- Writing/Scripting: 1.5 hours
- Video Production: 2-3 hours
- Editing: 1-2 hours
- Publishing/Optimization: 30 minutes
- Total: 6-8 hours per deep-dive

**Target**: 2-3 institution guides per week

### Pillar 2: How-To Guides (30% of content)

Educational content that helps students make decisions.

**Topic Ideas**:
- "How to Choose a College in 10 Steps"
- "Understanding Admission Requirements: Step-by-Step"
- "Scholarship Application Guide for Bangladeshi Students"
- "Online Learning Platforms: Complete Comparison"
- "Entrance Exam Preparation Timeline"
- "College vs. University: Key Differences"
- "Changing Your Major: What You Need to Know"

**Format**:
- YouTube: 5-7 minute tutorials
- Blog: 1,000-1,500 words
- Instagram: Educational carousel posts
- TikTok: Short tips (30-60 sec)
- Pinterest: Infographics

**Production Time**: 3-4 hours per guide

**Target**: 1-2 how-to guides per week

### Pillar 3: Student Stories (20% of content)

Real experiences from current/former students.

**Story Types**:
- "My College Transformation" - Before/after story
- "Why I Chose This College" - Decision-making journey
- "Student Life Vlog" - Day in the life
- "Placement Success" - Career outcome
- "Challenges & How I Overcame Them"
- "Best College Memories"
- "Advice for Future Students"

**Interview Process**:
1. Recruit: DM students on Instagram, ask ambassadors
2. Pre-interview: Brief call to discuss story
3. Shoot: 15-20 minute interview (video or text)
4. Edit: Compile into 5-10 minute video or 800-word blog
5. Publish: Credit student, link to app

**Production Time**: 4-6 hours (includes recruitment and interview)

**Target**: 1-2 student stories per week

### Pillar 4: Trending & News (10% of content)

Timely content on education news, policy changes, announcements.

**Content Types**:
- New university announcements or programs
- Admission deadline changes
- Policy updates affecting students
- Education news reactions
- Viral education trends on social media
- Institution milestones or rankings

**Turnaround Time**: 2-4 hours (quick turnaround)

**Target**: 2-3 per month or as needed

## Content Production Workflow

### Pre-Production

**Step 1: Planning (Brainstorm Session - 1 hour)**
- Review analytics from previous week
- Identify trending topics
- Gather content ideas from team, ambassadors, comments
- Create list of 10-15 content ideas

**Step 2: Topic Selection (30 minutes)**
- Prioritize by audience interest and strategic fit
- Assign production team
- Set deadline
- Gather resources

**Step 3: Research (30 minutes - 1.5 hours)**
- Gather information from primary sources
- Interview contacts if needed
- Verify facts and statistics
- Create research document/script outline

### Production

**Step 4: Script/Outline (45 minutes - 1 hour)**
- Write script (video) or outline (blog)
- Create talking points
- Add transitions and hooks
- Include calls-to-action

**Step 5: Recording/Writing (1-2 hours)**
- Shoot video or write blog post
- Get B-roll if needed
- Multiple takes for best quality
- Proofread content

**Step 6: Editing (1-2 hours)**
- Video: Cut, transitions, text overlays, music
- Blog: Formatting, images, internal links
- Optimize for platform specifications
- Add captions for video

### Post-Production

**Step 7: Optimization (30 minutes)**
- SEO: Keywords, meta descriptions, tags
- Platform specs: Dimensions, aspect ratios
- Hashtags and relevant tags
- Create thumbnail or featured image
- Write description with CTA

**Step 8: Review (15 minutes)**
- Quality check: spelling, grammar, facts
- Brand consistency: Logo, colors, messaging
- Platform compliance: No policy violations
- Approval sign-off

**Step 9: Publishing (15 minutes)**
- Schedule posts across platforms
- Set optimal posting times
- Monitor initial performance
- Pin/feature top content

**Step 10: Promotion (30 minutes - ongoing)**
- Share in community channels
- Tag relevant accounts
- Ambassador reshares
- Engagement response (comments, DMs)

## Platform-Specific Guidelines

### YouTube

**Best Practices**:
- Video length: 7-10 minutes (educational), 3-5 minutes (trending)
- Thumbnails: High contrast, faces, 1-2 colors, clear text
- Descriptions: 200+ words with timestamps and links
- Tags: 10-15 relevant keywords
- Playlist: Organize by series/topic

**CTA Strategy**:
- Subscribe button mention (0:15, 3:00, 7:00)
- End screen: Next video or subscribe button
- Description: "Download ShikkhaHub" with link
- Pinned comment: App link + CTA

**Upload Schedule**: 2-3 per week (Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday)

### TikTok

**Best Practices**:
- Video length: 15-60 seconds (longer in trending niches)
- Hook: First 2-3 seconds are critical (stop scroll)
- Trending sounds: Use what's trending in education niche
- Text overlays: 5-7 per video (keep it punchy)
- Dance/transitions: When appropriate, increases engagement

**Content Ideas**:
- Quick tips (15 sec): College myths busted, study hacks
- Trend participation: Educational takes on TikTok trends
- Student testimonials: 30-60 sec honest reviews
- Behind-the-scenes: Team, office, content creation
- Challenges: "Tag us in your college choice" etc.

**Upload Schedule**: Daily (morning 7-9 AM, afternoon 4-6 PM, night 8-10 PM)

### Instagram

**Best Practices**:
- Feed posts: Minimum 1 per day, ideally 2
- Stories: 3-5 per day (morning, afternoon, evening)
- Reels: 3-4 per week (Instagram algorithm favors)
- Captions: 150-300 words with emoji and line breaks
- Hashtags: 20-30 relevant hashtags (mix popular and niche)

**Content Mix**:
- 40% Educational (tips, guides, institution features)
- 30% User-generated content (student stories, reviews, ambassadors)
- 20% Behind-the-scenes (team, office, content creation)
- 10% Promotional (app features, new releases)

**Posting Schedule**:
- Feed: Mon/Wed/Fri 10 AM (optimal time)
- Stories: 3-5 daily throughout day
- Reels: Tue/Thu 10 AM

### Facebook

**Best Practices**:
- Post length: 200-300 words per post
- Images: 1.2:1.91 aspect ratio, minimum 1200x628px
- Videos: Auto-play enabled, captions included
- Engagement: Respond to all comments within 2 hours
- Groups: Active participation in 20+ relevant groups

**Content Strategy**:
- Longer-form articles and stories
- Community discussions and Q&A
- Event promotion and coverage
- News and announcements
- User testimonials and reviews

**Groups Strategy**:
- Join relevant groups (college, district-based, education)
- Share valuable content (not just promotional)
- Engage authentically with group members
- Build relationships with group admins
- Create monthly discussions/questions

**Posting Schedule**: 2-3 per day (morning 9 AM, afternoon 1 PM, evening 6 PM)

### Blog/Website

**Best Practices**:
- Post length: 1,000-2,000 words (optimal for SEO)
- Headers: Use H2/H3 tags properly
- Internal links: 3-5 links per article
- External links: Authoritative sources
- Images: 1 per 300 words minimum
- Meta description: 155-160 characters

**SEO Strategy**:
- Keyword research: Monthly refresh
- Long-tail keywords: "Best engineering colleges in Dhaka"
- Latent Semantic Indexing (LSI): Related keywords in content
- Schema markup: For institution pages
- Site speed: Monitor and optimize

**Publishing Schedule**: 3-4 posts per week (Mon/Wed/Fri/Sat)

## Content Batching & Efficiency

### Batch Recording Session (Monthly)

**Setup**: 1 day per month to record 4-5 videos

**Process**:
1. Set location and lighting
2. Record multiple institution guides (same setup)
3. Record supplementary content (intros, outros, CTAs)
4. B-roll: Campus footage, students, campus life
5. Interviews: Multiple students at once

**Output**: 
- 4-5 YouTube videos (2-3 weeks of content)
- TikTok clips from longer videos
- Instagram reels and carousel fodder
- Blog post source material

### Content Batching Benefits

- 50% time savings vs. individual recording sessions
- Consistent lighting/audio quality
- Efficient crew/equipment usage
- Easier scheduling with interview subjects
- Ability to repurpose content across platforms
