# Campus Ambassador Program - Complete System

## Program Overview

ShikkhaHub's Campus Ambassador Program leverages student leaders and influencers to drive organic growth through peer networks.

**Program Goals**:
- Recruit 50+ quality ambassadors
- Generate 5,000+ installs from ambassadors
- Build sustainable community growth
- Cost per install: $0.30-0.50

---

## Recruitment Pipeline

### Tier 1: Influencer Ambassadors (5-10)

**Profile**:
- 1,000+ social media followers
- Active in education/student communities
- Content creators (bloggers, YouTubers, Instagram influencers)
- Credible voice in education space

**Recruitment**:
- Direct outreach via Instagram/email
- Personalized pitch highlighting collaboration
- Higher compensation: $30-50/month + exclusive perks

**Incentives**:
- Monthly stipend: $30-50
- Product features (premium filters, advanced search)
- Co-branded content opportunities
- Exclusive merchandise
- Featured on app "Ambassador Spotlight"

### Tier 2: Student Leaders (15-25)

**Profile**:
- Student council members, club leaders
- 500-2,000 followers
- Active in campus communities
- Network of 50+ peers

**Recruitment**:
- Campus activation events
- Application form on website
- Referral from Tier 1 ambassadors
- Direct message outreach

**Incentives**:
- Monthly stipend: $15-20
- Performance bonuses: +$5 for 10+ installs
- Campus event funding: $50/quarter
- Prize eligibility
- Internal recognition/leaderboard

### Tier 3: Community Leaders (25-50)

**Profile**:
- Active in online communities (Reddit, Discord, Facebook groups)
- Regular contributors to discussion
- Known in their networks
- Education advocates

**Recruitment**:
- Community outreach
- Open application
- Referral program
- Word of mouth

**Incentives**:
- Monthly stipend: $10-15
- Performance bonus: +$3 for 5+ installs
- Exclusive digital badges
- Community recognition

## Onboarding Process

### Step 1: Application & Screening (Week 1)

**Application Form**:
- Basic info: Name, email, phone, institution
- Social presence: Follower count, links
- Motivation: Why they want to join
- Availability: Hours per week
- Experience: Previous ambassador/referral experience

**Screening**:
- Review profile quality
- Check social media authenticity
- Background check basics
- Approval within 3-5 days

### Step 2: Ambassador Training (Week 1-2)

**Training Materials** (1-2 hours):
- Handbook: 20-page PDF
- Video onboarding: 15 minutes
- Values & guidelines
- How-to guides
- Content templates
- FAQs

**Training Topics**:
- ShikkhaHub's mission and features
- Target audience (high school to graduate students)
- Ethical promotion (no spam, genuine recommendations)
- Social media best practices
- FAQ and objection handling
- Performance metrics and rewards

### Step 3: Setup (Week 2)

**Tools & Access**:
- Unique referral link (tracks installs)
- Content templates and assets
- Ambassador Discord community invite
- Monthly reporting dashboard access
- T-shirt and welcome package shipped

**First 7 Days**:
- Make first 3 social posts
- Share referral link 3 times
- Join Ambassador community
- Attend kickoff call
- Submit feedback form

## Events & Activation

### Campus Activation Day (Monthly per ambassador)

**Format**: 2-3 hour campus event
**Funding**: $50-100 per event
**Setup**: Table + signage
**Activities**:
- App demos on phone
- QR code registration
- Giveaways (t-shirts, stickers)
- Instagram photo booth

**Promotion**:
- Ambassador shares event on social media (7 days before)
- Event flyer (100 copies)
- Campus announcement boards
- WhatsApp groups

**Execution**:
- Ambassador runs demo booth
- Screenshots of students downloading
- Collect photos/testimonials for content
- Report attendance and installs

**Success Metrics**:
- Attendance: 20-50 students
- Installs: 5-15 per event
- Social reach: 500-2,000 impressions

### Referral Challenge (Quarterly)

**Theme**: "Refer 5 Friends Challenge"
**Duration**: 2 weeks
**Prizes**:
- 1st Place: $100 cash + exclusive merch
- 2nd Place: $50 cash + t-shirt
- 3rd Place: $25 cash

**Mechanics**:
- Ambassador shares unique link
- Tracks referrals in real-time
- Leaderboard updated daily

**Expected Results**:
- 200-500 installs from 50 ambassadors
- 20% participation rate

## Ambassador Guidelines

### Do's ✅

- Be authentic and genuine in recommendations
- Share your real experience with the app
- Engage with comments and questions
- Attend ambassador calls and training
- Report issues and provide feedback
- Celebrate other ambassadors' wins
- Create diverse content types
- Have fun and be creative!

### Don'ts ❌

- Spam or aggressive promotion
- Misleading claims about features
- Posting without authentic interest
- Ignoring feedback or guidelines
- Promoting competitors
- Fake reviews or fake install tracking
- Violating social media platform policies
- Missing deadlines for campaigns
