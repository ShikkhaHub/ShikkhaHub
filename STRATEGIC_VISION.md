## Why This Plan Works

### The Data Strategy
1. Start with **official sources only** (UGC, Boards, BTEB)
   - Trust score: 0.95+
   - No bad data from day 1

2. Expand with **verified sources** (alumni, partnerships)
   - Trust score: 0.8-0.9
   - Crowdsourced verification

3. Fill gaps with **web scraping** (institutions' websites)
   - Trust score: 0.7-0.8
   - Low priority, fills holes

**Result:** 95%+ verified database by Month 6

### The Distribution Strategy
1. **Organic (Month 1-3):** Official channels
   - Government announcements
   - University partnerships
   - Media coverage

2. **Ambassadors (Month 3-6):** Campus networks
   - 50+ ambassadors by Month 5
   - Each reaches 1,000+ students
   - 50K organic installs

3. **Paid (Month 6+):** Google/Facebook ads
   - Only after organic growth
   - Capture search demand
   - <$2 CAC

### The AI Strategy
Not a chatbot. It's a **decision engine** that:
- Reads actual institution data (not hallucination)
- Calculates real admission probability
- Links to verified programs
- Provides actionable recommendations
- Shows confidence levels
