# Growth Channels Strategy

## Overview

ShikkhaHub's growth strategy focuses on multiple channels to reach students across Bangladesh. Target: 50,000 DAU by Month 6.

## 1. Ambassador Program (Primary Channel)

### Target
- 500 active ambassadors by Month 6
- 50% of new user signups from ambassador referrals

### Structure
```
Bronze Tier    → 1-10 referrals    → Badge + listing
Silver Tier    → 11-50 referrals   → Merchandise + exclusive content
Gold Tier      → 51-150 referrals  → Cash rewards ($50-200)
Platinum Tier  → 150+ referrals    → Campus ambassador role + salary
```

### Ambassador Perks
- Unique referral link with tracking
- Leaderboard ranking
- Monthly contests and prizes
- Exclusive ambassador Discord community
- Early access to new features
- Free ShikkhaHub Premium account

### Recruitment Strategy
- Automated bot in WhatsApp groups
- In-app ambassador application
- LinkedIn outreach to student leaders
- Email campaigns to engaged users
- Campus ambassador events

### Referral Incentives
- Ambassador: Get 100 points per referral
- Referred User: Get 50 points (first purchase discount)
- Bonus: If 5 friends signup in month, get 500 bonus points

## 2. Social Media (Secondary Channel)

### Platforms & Strategy

**Facebook (Target: 100K followers)**
- Daily institution spotlights
- Student testimonials
- Study tips and college life advice
- University news and announcements
- Weekly live Q&A sessions

**Instagram (Target: 50K followers)**
- Campus photography and reels
- Student stories and testimonials
- Tips and educational content
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Stories with engagement polls

**TikTok (Target: 30K followers)**
- Short-form campus tours
- Student day-in-the-life videos
- College mythology myths/truths
- Motivational student stories
- Fun educational content

**YouTube (Target: 10K subscribers)**
- Full campus tours (5-10 min)
- Student Q&A sessions
- Admission process guides
- College comparison videos
- Student experience vlogs

### Content Calendar

**Monthly Themes:**
- Month 1: "Why ShikkhaHub?" (product education)
- Month 2: "College Life Myths" (viral content)
- Month 3: "Top Performers" (ambassador stories)
- Month 4: "Admission Guide" (evergreen content)
- Month 5: "Success Stories" (student testimonials)
- Month 6: "College Ranking" (native content)

### Content Guidelines
- Post 3x daily on Facebook
- Post 1-2x daily on Instagram (1 main + 3-5 stories)
- 3-4 TikToks per week
- 2-3 YouTube videos per month
- Always include call-to-action (download link)

## 4. Paid Advertising

### Budget Allocation: $2,000/month

```
Facebook/Instagram Ads    : $800
Google Search             : $500
Google Display            : $400
YouTube Pre-roll          : $300
```

### Facebook/Instagram Strategy
- Targeting: Students 15-25 interested in education
- Creatives: Campus photos, student testimonials, feature highlights
- Lookalike audiences: Based on app installers
- Budget: $800/month = ~$3 CPC

### Google Search Strategy
- Bidding on high-intent keywords
- Target: "colleges in bangladesh", "university admissions"
- Budget: $500/month

### Success Metrics
- CPA (Cost Per Acquisition): < $3
- ROAS (Return on Ad Spend): > 300%
- CTR: > 2%

## 5. Email Marketing

### Email Segments

**Segment 1: Non-users**
- Goal: Drive first signup
- Frequency: 2x week
- Content: Product benefits, social proof

**Segment 2: Casual users** (< 2 searches/month)
- Goal: Increase engagement
- Frequency: 2x week
- Content: Personalized recommendations, tips

**Segment 3: Active users** (> 5 searches/month)
- Goal: Monetization & retention
- Frequency: 1x week
- Content: Premium features, community events

**Segment 4: Ambassadors**
- Goal: Drive referrals
- Frequency: 1x week
- Content: Leaderboard updates, contests, tips

## 9. Analytics & Measurement

- LTV (Lifetime Value)
